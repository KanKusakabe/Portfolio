About

Hi, I'm Kan Kusakabe, a second‑year PhD student in Information Science at Hokkaido University and an HCI researcher. My work centers on interactive mobile interfaces (accessory‑style designs, advanced sensing, and gesture input). By combining machine learning with rapid prototyping and 3D printing, I create solutions that seamlessly blend digital and physical experiences. I'm always eager to discuss research collaborations, internships, or career opportunities. Thanks for visiting my portfolio, and feel free to reach out!
